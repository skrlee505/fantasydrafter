POWER MODEL

How the score works

Power Score is relative to this league and snapshot. It combines projected legal starters (45%), roster depth (15%), completed performance (20%), record (10%), and availability (10%).

  • Every player can fill only one legal lineup slot per week.
  • Bench points count only when that player replaces a starter.
  • All-play results separate weekly performance from matchup schedule.
  • Evidence confidence is separate from team quality. Missing starter data can withhold a score.

Scores cannot be compared across unrelated leagues or formula versions. They are decision support, not playoff or championship odds.
