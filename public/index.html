TRANSPARENT MODEL

Why this order?

The score blends league-adjusted projection, value over replacement, tier scarcity, roster fit, next-pick availability, player risk, ADP, upside, and modest stack value.

  • Starter safety leads early; ceiling gains weight for bench picks.
  • Hero RB is a preference, not a rule.
  • QB and TE are delayed unless elite-tier value is available.
  • Kicker and defense are held for the final rounds.
Prototype projections are a bundled demonstration dataset. Live Sleeper scoring and picks are loaded when available; projection-provider provenance and freshness are disclosed in the UI.

Draft articles

Import draft-strategy articles separately from player rankings. Draftside detects supported advice and applies a limited, visible adjustment to the recommendation engine.

Supports text, Markdown, HTML, and text-searchable PDF files. The complete extracted source is saved in the same durable on-Mac library as ranking sources. Image-only scanned PDFs need OCR first.
